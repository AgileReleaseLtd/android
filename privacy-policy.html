Privacy Policy — 2048 Cosmos

Last updated: 7 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2048 Cosmos (the "Game", package uk.co.agilerelease.game2048), published by Agile Release, handles information when you play. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The Game does not require an account and we do not ask you for your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your progress (scores, unlocked stages, settings) is stored locally on your device.
  • Third‑party Google services we use — ads, Google Play Games, and in‑app purchases — collect limited data as described below.

Information collected

We do not directly collect personal information. The following third‑party services integrated into the Game may collect data:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows banner and rewarded ads via the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier, approximate (IP‑based) location, app interactions, and device/diagnostic information. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we present a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before requesting personalised ads. See Google's practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games Services associates your gameplay (scores on leaderboards, achievements) with your Play Games player ID so those features work. Signing in is optional; the Game is fully playable without it. See Google's Privacy Policy.

In‑app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (e.g. removing ads or power‑ups) are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details; Google provides us only with the purchase status needed to deliver what you bought.

How data is used

  • To display and measure advertising, and to support ad‑supported free play.
  • To provide optional leaderboards and achievements.
  • To process optional in‑app purchases.
  • To keep the Game working correctly and diagnose problems.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data described above is processed by the relevant Google service (AdMob, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing) acting as an independent controller or processor under Google's own terms. We do not share data with any other third parties.

Your choices

  • Ads personalisation: In the EEA/UK/Switzerland you can change consent via the in‑game privacy option. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Play Games: You can play without signing in, and manage Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.
  • Local data: Uninstalling the Game removes locally stored progress.
  • Data deletion: To request deletion of data associated with the Game, email us (below).

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured accordingly where required.

Security & retention

Data transmitted by the Game and its Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Retention of data collected by Google services is governed by Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
