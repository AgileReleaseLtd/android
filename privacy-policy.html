Privacy Policy — Agile Release Games

Effective 7 July 2026 · Last updated 7 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games published by Agile Release Limited ("Agile Release", "we", "us") on Google Play (each, "the Game") — including 2048 Cosmos, Word Search, and our other Android titles. By using one of our Games you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • Our Games do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your progress (scores, levels, settings) is stored locally on your device.
  • Third-party Google services we use — ads, Google Play Games, and in-app purchases — collect limited data as described below.
  • We run no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

1. Who we are

These Games are published by Agile Release Limited, a company registered in England and Wales, registered office: Agile Release Limited, 7 Larkshall Road, London, E4 7HS, United Kingdom. For any privacy question or request, contact info@agilerelease.co.uk.

2. What data the Games process

2.1 Stored only on your device

Game progress and settings (saves, best scores, streaks, unlocked content, sound/tutorial preferences) are stored locally via Android app storage and removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. This never leaves your device.

2.2 Leaderboards & achievements (Google Play Games Services, optional)

Our Games offer optional leaderboards and achievements via Google Play Games Services. If you choose to sign in, Google associates your gameplay (best scores, achievements) with your Google Play Games player ID so those features work. Signing in is optional — every Game is fully playable without it, and if you never sign in, no leaderboard/achievement data is collected. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy; manage or delete it in your Google Play Games settings.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our Games are free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect your advertising identifier, device and network information, app interactions, and general (IP-based) location. See Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): we show Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before personalised ads load.
  • Your controls: reset or limit your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Removing ads: where offered, a one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase disables ads in that Game.

2.4 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (e.g. Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details; the app only records, on your device, what you have purchased so it can unlock and restore content.

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise GPS location, or biometric data, and we run no first-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with Google — AdMob (advertising), Google Play Billing (purchases), and Google Play Games Services (leaderboards & achievements), each acting under its own privacy policy. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the service.

5. Data retention & deletion

  • On-device data — remains until you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.
  • Leaderboard/achievement data — held by Google Play Games under Google's retention policies; manage or delete it in your Play Games settings, or email us.
  • Advertising data — governed by Google's retention policies.

To request deletion of data associated with our Games, see our Data Deletion Request page or email info@agilerelease.co.uk.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (UK/EEA under UK GDPR/GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, etc.) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port your personal data. As most data is on your device or held by Google, the fastest route is to clear the app's data / uninstall or use your Google account controls. For anything else, contact info@agilerelease.co.uk. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

7. Children

The Games are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; where required, ad personalisation is disabled for users indicated as children.

8. International transfers

Google's services may process data outside the UK/EEA under appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) as described in Google's privacy documentation.
